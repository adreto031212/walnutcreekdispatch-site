Who we are and how we work.

Who publishes the Walnut Creek Dispatch

Sponsors

Every paid placement carries the word "Sponsored". Ads never buy coverage. No real estate brokerages or agents and no political ads as sponsors.

Sources

Every item comes from a public source (city and county agencies, school districts, transit agencies, parks departments, the organizer's own page) and links it. The issues are researched and written with AI tools, and an editor reads every issue before it goes out.

Your information

Your email is used to send the Walnut Creek Dispatch and nothing else. It is never sold, never shared and never given to advertisers. Unsubscribe in one click at the bottom of any issue.

Corrections

Tell us by replying to any issue or writing to hello@walnutcreekdispatch.com. Corrections run in the next issue and on the web copy within a day.

Contact

Victoria Muller, hello@walnutcreekdispatch.com, for readers, sponsors and anything else. Mail: Neighborhood Press LLC, 11201 SE 8th St, Ste 152, Bellevue, WA 98004.
