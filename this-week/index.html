Walnut Creek and Lamorinda · Nov 19 to Nov 25

This week in Walnut Creek and Lamorinda.

The dated things to know between now and next Thursday, from issue 1 of the Walnut Creek Dispatch. Every item links its public source.

Heads up

  • BART Yellow Line, Lafayette to Walnut Creek, from midnight on the work nights of November 19 and 20. BART runs on one track between the two stations for rail work; expect delays of 20 to 30 minutes on late trains. BART
  • Treat Boulevard at the Iron Horse Trail bridge, night lane closures through November 25. County crews work 9 PM to 5 AM, Sunday to Thursday; trail users cross at the street while the bridge is closed. More below. Contra Costa County
  • Lafayette Reservoir, no cars Monday to Wednesday, November 23 to 25, 6:30 AM to 2 PM. The walking path stays open during park hours. More below. EBMUD

What the city decided and what changed

EBMUD pulls its construction equipment out of Lafayette Reservoir next week

The work is the seismic retrofit of the reservoir's outlet tower, which is also the dam's spillway.

Orinda is asking where its sidewalks, bike routes and trails fall short

The city is updating its Bicycle, Trails and Walkways Master Plan for the first time since 2011.

Walnut Creek's Transportation Commission meets tonight at 6 PM at City Hall, 1666 North Main Street

It is collecting ideas for the City Council's 2027–2028 priorities, which the council sets at a goal-setting session on March 9, 2027.

The Ygnacio Valley Library stays closed until about May 2027

Contra Costa County is replacing its roof and heating and cooling.

sandbags before the rain

Walnut Creek stocks five sandbag stations through the winter: Lancaster Road near Orchard Lane, Heather Farm Park by ballfields 5 and 6, and the Larkey, Howe Homestead and Rudgear park lots.
