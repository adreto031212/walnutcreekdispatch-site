Walnut Creek and Lamorinda · One useful thing

sandbags before the rain

November 19, 2026 · Walnut Creek Dispatch, issue 1 · All Walnut Creek and Lamorinda news

Walnut Creek stocks five sandbag stations through the winter: Lancaster Road near Orchard Lane, Heather Farm Park by ballfields 5 and 6, and the Larkey, Howe Homestead and Rudgear park lots. Sand and bags are free; bring your own shovel. A bag can be kept under cover for the next storm until the plastic weave breaks down. City of Walnut Creek In Moraga, the station is in the Rancho Laguna Park lot near the restrooms, shovels included, for Moraga residents. Town of Moraga
